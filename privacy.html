Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Juan Pedro Huici ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you purchase or use SessionTide (the "Software") or visit sessiontide.com (the "Site"), and how we use, share, and protect it.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you purchase a license, we (or our payment processor, on our behalf) collect your name, email address, and payment information necessary to complete the transaction and deliver your license key.

Information the Software collects. SessionTide runs locally on your Mac and communicates with Pro Tools on the same machine via Avid's Pro Tools Scripting SDK (PTSL) and, for some cleanup categories, local AppleScript/System Events automation. To identify what to clean up, the Software reads certain information already present in your session, including:

  • File paths;
  • Session or project names;
  • Clip, track, or marker names;
  • Plugin and preset names;
  • Other metadata needed to identify items for cleanup.

None of this ever leaves your Mac. It is not transmitted to us, to Gumroad, or to any other party, and we do not store it anywhere.

The only data sent over the network by the Software is for license activation: your license key, sent to Gumroad's License Verification API solely to verify that the key is valid and has not been refunded or disputed. No session content, file paths, or device-identifying information is ever included in that check. The Software sends nothing besides this license check — no crash reports, no usage analytics, no telemetry.

Information collected automatically on the Site. Like most websites, the Site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and, if enabled, analytics (none at this time). This may include your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Process your purchase and deliver your license key;
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries;
  • Send you important notices about the Software (e.g., security updates), and, only with your consent, product news;
  • Detect and prevent fraud, license abuse, or misuse of the Site.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Gumroad, to process payments and prevent fraud;
  • Law enforcement or other parties when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain purchase records for as long as needed to provide support, honor refund requests, and comply with tax and accounting obligations (typically seven (7) years). You may request deletion of your personal information subject to Section 7 below.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the GDPR if you are in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA if you are a California resident).
